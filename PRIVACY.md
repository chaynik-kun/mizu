# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 24, 2026**

Mizu is an open-source Android music client for Navidrome and OpenSubsonic-compatible servers.

This Privacy Policy explains how Mizu handles information when you use the application.

## Overview

Mizu is designed as a client for a music server chosen and configured by the user.

Mizu does not operate a cloud service for storing your music library, listening history, account credentials, or other personal data.

Mizu does not sell user data.

Mizu does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs.

## Server Connections

To use Mizu, you provide connection information for your Navidrome or OpenSubsonic-compatible server. This may include:

- Server URL
- Username
- Password or authentication credentials
- Custom HTTP headers, if configured by the user

This information is used to establish a connection between Mizu and the server selected by the user.

Authentication and server information may be stored locally on the user's device as necessary for the application to maintain the configured server connection.

Mizu does not send your server credentials to a Mizu-operated server.

The server you connect to may independently collect or process information according to its own configuration and privacy practices. Mizu does not control servers operated by users or third parties.

## Music Library and Playback Data

Mizu accesses information provided by your configured music server in order to provide its functionality. Depending on the server, this may include:

- Artists
- Albums
- Songs
- Playlists
- Album artwork
- Lyrics
- Playback information
- Favorites
- Listening history
- Other media metadata provided by the server

This information is processed by Mizu to display and play your music library and provide related application features.

Some information may be stored locally on your device for caching, offline playback, downloads, application state, or performance purposes.

Mizu does not upload your music library to a Mizu-operated cloud service.

## Downloads and Automatic Caching

Mizu can download or automatically cache media and related data on your device.

Downloaded and cached content remains on the device unless it is removed by the user, removed through Mizu's storage controls, cleared by Android, or removed when the application is uninstalled, depending on the type of stored data.

## Scrobbling and Third-Party Services

Mizu may support optional integrations with third-party scrobbling or music services.

If you enable such an integration, information required to provide that feature may be transmitted to the service selected by you. Depending on the integration, this may include information such as:

- Track title
- Artist
- Album
- Playback progress
- Playback timestamps or listening activity

These integrations are optional and are only used when configured or enabled by the user.

Information transmitted to a third-party service is subject to that service's own privacy policy and terms. Mizu does not control how those third-party services process information.

## Cast, DLNA and UPnP

Mizu supports playback on compatible devices using technologies such as Cast, DLNA and UPnP.

When these features are used, Mizu may discover compatible devices on your local network and communicate with the device selected by you.

Information necessary for playback, such as media metadata, playback commands, media locations, or related connection information, may be transmitted to the selected playback device or service as necessary to perform the requested playback.

Local network discovery and communication are used for this functionality and are not used by Mizu for advertising or user tracking.

## Android Auto

Mizu supports Android Auto.

When Android Auto is used, media information and playback controls may be made available to Android Auto as necessary to provide the in-car music experience.

## Local Storage

Mizu stores certain information locally on your device to provide application functionality. This may include:

- Application preferences
- Server configuration
- Authentication information
- Cached music and artwork
- Downloaded music
- Playback state
- Queue information
- Other application state necessary for Mizu to operate

This information is not maintained in a Mizu-operated cloud account.

## Network Security

Mizu supports secure HTTPS connections to compatible servers and users are encouraged to use HTTPS whenever possible.

Mizu may also allow users to explicitly connect to servers using unencrypted HTTP, which may be useful for certain self-hosted or local network configurations.

Unencrypted HTTP connections do not provide transport encryption. Data transmitted over such a connection, potentially including authentication information and media traffic, may be intercepted or modified by parties with access to the network.

Mizu displays a warning when the user chooses to allow an insecure HTTP connection.

## Analytics and Advertising

Mizu does not include advertising.

Mizu does not use user information for advertising or marketing profiling.

Mizu does not intentionally track users across applications or websites.

If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or other telemetry services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before those services are used where required.

## Data Sharing

Mizu does not sell personal information.

Mizu does not operate a backend service to which your music library or listening activity is routinely uploaded.

Information may leave your device when necessary to provide functionality explicitly requested or configured by you, including communication with:

- Your configured Navidrome or OpenSubsonic-compatible server
- Third-party services that you choose to enable
- Cast, DLNA, UPnP, Android Auto, or other playback devices and services used by you

Those systems and services may have their own privacy practices.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because Mizu does not operate user accounts or a cloud service for storing Mizu user data, there is no Mizu cloud account to delete.

Information stored locally by Mizu can be removed by:

- Removing downloaded or cached content using available application controls
- Clearing Mizu's application data through Android system settings
- Uninstalling Mizu

Data stored or processed by your Navidrome/OpenSubsonic server or an optional third-party service must be managed through that server or service.

## Permissions

Mizu may request Android permissions or system access necessary to provide features such as network connectivity, local network device discovery, media playback, notifications, and related functionality.

Permissions are used only for their associated application functionality.

## Children's Privacy

Mizu is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

Mizu does not knowingly operate a service that collects personal information from children.
