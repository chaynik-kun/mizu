# Acknowledgements

## Navic provenance

Mizu is based on [Navic](https://github.com/ssalggnikool/Navic), created by Paige and developed with the
Navic contributors. The Mizu source tree began from Navic revision
`cf720ef99592e6bf2de3a25c3458034a3444e8d6` and has since received substantial Android-focused changes.
Mizu is a modified version of Navic, and the substantial Mizu modifications documented here were made in 2026.

Major categories of Mizu modifications and additions include:

- Android playback and Media3 integration improvements;
- network and server-connection handling;
- automatic playback caching and next-track pre-buffering;
- Android Auto browsing, settings, and search handling;
- DLNA/UPnP remote playback;
- Android system equalizer integration;
- UI/UX changes, lifecycle fixes, and low-end performance work.

The Git history of Navic and Mizu is the authoritative contributor record. This acknowledgement does not
replace or limit any copyright notice or license term from the original project.
